## STEP 3 — GAP ANALYSIS

### Gap Type A: Filer in Transcript, NOT in SFH Records
- Payer filed a 1099 against your SSN but you have no account documentation
- **Action:** Formal records request; attorney demand if dispute

### Gap Type B: Known Account, NO 1099 Filed
- You have statements showing activity but IRS shows no 1099 from that filer
- **Action:** Investigate — broker may have failed to report (IRC §6045 violation) or account may have been improperly closed before reporting

### Gap Type C: 1099 Amount vs. Delta CSV Discrepancy
- IRS transcript shows different proceeds/income than Delta CSV data
- **Action:** Flag for CPA; may indicate missing transactions, incorrect cost basis, or broker reporting error

### Gap Type D: Disputed Account Filed a 1099
- NFS/Plynk, TD Ameritrade, or Wells Fargo filed a 1099 for proceeds SFH never received
- **Action:** This is direct evidence — assign exhibit number, route to attorney and FINRA filing
